Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect only the information necessary to provide the Service:

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter manually.
  • Usage data: basic logs such as login timestamps and page visits, used solely for security and debugging.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data from your device beyond what you explicitly provide.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Display your expense history and spending summaries within the app.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Diagnose technical issues and improve the reliability of the Service.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the Service for you.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secured servers. We apply standard security practices including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls to protect your information.

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Upon deletion, your data will be permanently removed from our systems within 30 days.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly uses a small number of third-party services to operate:

  • Google Fonts: fonts are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log the request; see Google's Privacy Policy for details.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third party for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You may also request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

6. Contact Us

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out through the contact information provided on our site.

For details on how you may use the Service, please also review our Terms and Conditions.
